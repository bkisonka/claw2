# CLA (individual) — copyright assignment agreement for contributions to claw2

Version 1.0 · 2026-11-21 · Project: [claw2](https://github.com/bkisonka/claw2)
Project copyright holder: Vitaliy Bondar (GitHub: [bkisonka](https://github.com/bkisonka))

> **This English text is the legally binding version of the agreement.**
> A Russian translation is provided for convenience only:
> [CLA-individual.ru.md](CLA-individual.ru.md).

Fill in the "Signature" block at the end of this document and submit the
file as a single commit to the `cla-signatures` branch. Committing the
filled-in file is recognized as signing in simple electronic written form.
One signature covers all your future contributions to claw2; you do not
need to sign again.

## 1. Parties and definitions

- **Project copyright holder** — Vitaliy Bondar, the copyright holder of
  the claw2 project.
- **Author** — an individual who has signed this agreement.
- **Contribution** — any work of authorship submitted by the Author to the
  claw2 project (via a pull request, an issue with a patch, or otherwise):
  code, documentation, tests, translations, graphics, edits to existing
  materials.

## 2. Copyright assignment

2.1. The Author hereby assigns to the Project copyright holder the
exclusive right in each Contribution in full ("all right, title and
interest"). The exclusive right passes to the Project copyright holder upon
submission of the Contribution to the project, without limitation of
territory or term (Article 1234 of the Civil Code of the Russian
Federation).

2.2. This agreement is gratuitous (no compensation is payable).

2.3. Moral rights (the right of authorship and other personal
non-property rights) remain with the Author and are not assigned; the
Author's name is preserved in the repository history (Articles 1228 and
1265 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation).

2.4. The Project copyright holder may use the Contributions in any manner
and dispose of the exclusive right in them without limitation, including:
distributing the Contributions as part of claw2 under **AGPL-3.0-only**
(the HSS core files — under **LGPL-3.0-only**); distributing them under
separate commercial licenses; making modifications and combining them with
other works.

## 3. Back-up license

If the assignment under Section 2 is found invalid or inapplicable with
respect to a particular Contribution, the Author unconditionally grants the
Project copyright holder, with respect to that Contribution, a perpetual,
irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to all
exclusive rights, including the right to sublicense and to distribute the
Contribution under any licenses. This section is a back-up mechanism and
applies only if Section 2 is invalid.

## 4. Patents

If a Contribution is covered by a patent owned by the Author, the Author
grants the Project copyright holder a royalty-free, irrevocable, perpetual,
worldwide license to use such patents with respect to the Contribution and
its derivatives as part of claw2 — including commercial use.

## 5. Representations

The Author represents and warrants that:

- the Author is the sole owner of the rights in the Contribution, or is
  entitled to assign them in full;
- the Contribution contains no third-party materials whose rights the
  Author is not entitled to assign, and does not infringe third-party
  rights;
- if the Contribution was created within (or is related to) the Author's
  employment or service relationships, the Author has obtained all
  necessary consents from the employer;
- the Contribution contains no confidential information or protected
  secrets of third parties.

## 6. The Author's rights after assignment

The Project copyright holder grants the Author a perpetual, irrevocable,
royalty-free license to use the Author's own Contributions — including as
part of the Author's other projects.

## 7. Form, entry into force and withdrawal

7.1. Filling in the "Signature" block and committing the signed file to the
`cla-signatures` branch is recognized as conclusion of this agreement in
simple electronic written form (Article 434 of the Civil Code of the
Russian Federation) and confirms the Author's intent.

7.2. The agreement enters into force upon the commit and applies to all
Contributions submitted after that moment. If the signature lists links to
earlier submitted PRs/issues, the agreement extends to them as well.

7.3. The Author may terminate the agreement for future Contributions by
notifying the Project copyright holder. The assignment of the exclusive
right for Contributions already submitted to the project is irrevocable.

## 8. Governing law and disputes

This agreement is governed by the law of the Russian Federation. Disputes
are considered by a court at the location of the Project copyright holder,
unless the parties agree on a different procedure.
