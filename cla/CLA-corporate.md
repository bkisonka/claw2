# CLA (corporate) — copyright assignment agreement for company contributions to claw2

Version 1.0 · 2026-11-21 · Project: [claw2](https://github.com/bkisonka/claw2)
Project copyright holder: Vitaliy Bondar (GitHub: [bkisonka](https://github.com/bkisonka))

> **This English text is the legally binding version of the agreement.**
> A Russian translation is provided for convenience only:
> [CLA-corporate.ru.md](CLA-corporate.ru.md).

Signed by an organization whose employees submit contributions to claw2
(including work code). Fill in the company details and the "Signature"
block, attach the list of authorized employees (Annex A), and submit the
file as a single commit to the `cla-signatures` branch. Updates to the
employee list are made by a separate commit to the same branch (Annex A
only).

## 1. Parties and definitions

- **Project copyright holder** — Vitaliy Bondar, the copyright holder of
  the claw2 project.
- **Company** — a legal entity that has signed this agreement (details in
  the "Signature" block).
- **Authorized employee** — an individual listed in Annex A.
- **Contribution** — any work of authorship submitted to the claw2 project
  by an Authorized employee on behalf of the Company: code, documentation,
  tests, translations, graphics, edits to existing materials.

## 2. Copyright assignment

2.1. The Company hereby assigns to the Project copyright holder the
exclusive right in each Contribution in full ("all right, title and
interest"). The exclusive right passes to the Project copyright holder upon
submission of the Contribution to the project, without limitation of
territory or term (Article 1234 of the Civil Code of the Russian
Federation).

2.2. This agreement is gratuitous (no compensation is payable).

2.3. Moral rights (the right of authorship and other personal
non-property rights) of Authorized employees remain with them and are not
assigned; the authors' names are preserved in the repository history
(Articles 1228 and 1265 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation).

2.4. The Project copyright holder may use the Contributions in any manner
and dispose of the exclusive right in them without limitation, including:
distributing the Contributions as part of claw2 under **AGPL-3.0-only**
(the HSS core files — under **LGPL-3.0-only**); distributing them under
separate commercial licenses; making modifications and combining them with
other works.

## 3. Back-up license

If the assignment under Section 2 is found invalid or inapplicable with
respect to a particular Contribution, the Company unconditionally grants
the Project copyright holder, with respect to that Contribution, a
perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to
all exclusive rights, including the right to sublicense and to distribute
the Contribution under any licenses. This section is a back-up mechanism
and applies only if Section 2 is invalid.

## 4. Patents

If a Contribution is covered by a patent owned by the Company, the Company
grants the Project copyright holder a royalty-free, irrevocable, perpetual,
worldwide license to use such patents with respect to the Contribution and
its derivatives as part of claw2 — including commercial use.

## 5. Representations

The Company represents and warrants that:

- it is the employer of the Authorized employees and holds the rights in
  Contributions created by them within the scope of their duties
  (Article 1295 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation), or has
  acquired the rights in the Contributions in another sufficient manner;
- the Contributions contain no third-party materials whose rights the
  Company is not entitled to assign, and do not infringe third-party
  rights;
- the Contributions contain no confidential information or protected
  secrets of third parties;
- the list in Annex A is correct and is kept up to date as the set of
  Authorized employees changes.

Contributions submitted by employees who are not listed in the current
Annex A are not deemed transferred under this agreement.

## 6. The Company's rights after assignment

The Project copyright holder grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable,
royalty-free license to use the Contributions — including as part of the
Company's own products.

## 7. Form, entry into force and withdrawal

7.1. Filling in the "Signature" block and committing the signed file to the
`cla-signatures` branch is recognized as conclusion of this agreement in
simple electronic written form (Article 434 of the Civil Code of the
Russian Federation) and confirms the Company's intent; the signature of the
signatory confirms the consent of the Company's lawful representative.

7.2. The agreement enters into force upon the commit and applies to all
Contributions submitted after that moment. If the signature lists links to
earlier submitted PRs/issues, the agreement extends to them as well.

7.3. The Company may terminate the agreement for future Contributions by
notifying the Project copyright holder, and may update Annex A at any time.
The assignment of the exclusive right for Contributions already submitted
to the project is irrevocable.

## 8. Governing law and disputes

This agreement is governed by the law of the Russian Federation. Disputes
are considered by a court at the location of the Project copyright holder,
unless the parties agree on a different procedure.
